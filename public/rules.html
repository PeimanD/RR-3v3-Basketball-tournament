Game rules

3v3 half court. These rules are written for 5 teams on 2 courts.

  • 12minute cap per game
  • 15points to win
  • 2s & 3sinside and beyond the arc

Games

  • A game ends when a team reaches 15 or the 12-minute clock runs out, whichever comes first.
  • If the score is tied when time runs out, play on: the next basket wins. Every game has a winner.
  • Baskets count 2 inside the arc and 3 beyond it. Many games will end before the 12-minute cap.
  • Every team plays every other team once. Two games run at a time and one team sits each round.

Standings and tiebreakers

Teams are ranked by wins. Points can be gamed or misleading, so they only break ties:

  1. Wins
  2. Head-to-head result (two-team ties only)
  3. Point differential. Games end at 15, so blowouts can’t distort it much.

If teams are still level after that: points scored, then a coin flip. When a tiebreaker splits a three-way tie and two teams are still level, those two go back to head-to-head.

Playoffs

Everyone plays. Three rounds, about 45 minutes.

Round 1

Seed 2 vs seed 3, and seed 4 vs seed 5.

The 1 seed rests, a reward for winning the pool.

Round 2

Seed 1 vs the winner of 4 vs 5. The two round 1 losers play a consolation game.

The winner of 2 vs 3 rests.

Round 3

Final: the winner of 2 vs 3 against the winner of seed 1’s round 2 game.

On the other court, the 3rd place game: the loser of seed 1’s round 2 game against the consolation winner.

On the court

Possession

  • After a made basket, possession is either make-it-take-it or alternating. The organizer announces which before the first game.
  • After a defensive rebound or a steal, clear the ball past the arc before you can score.
  • Restart dead balls with a check at the top.

Fouls

  • Players call their own fouls.
  • Team foul bonus, if the organizer turns it on: from a team’s 6th foul in a game onward, each foul gives the other team a bonus possession.

Clock and score

  • The sitting team runs both clocks and both scoreboards.
